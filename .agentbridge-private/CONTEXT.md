## Latest confirmed scope

- AgentBridge is a protocol and SDK ecosystem, not an agent, user interface, shopping agent, or proprietary aggregator.
- Its intended core is role-neutral and direction-neutral: agent↔service, agent↔agent, service↔service/B2B, reverse asynchronous flows, multi-party/delegated chains, and participant interaction with replaceable discovery, trust, policy, and audit infrastructure through explicit bindings must be representable regardless of participant ownership.
- Organizational labels are context, not permanent protocol roles. The future model must evaluate dynamic roles including principal, initiator/requester, delegate, responder/provider, executor, approver, resource owner, policy authority, and attestor.
- The agent-to-service consequential-action flow is the first high-risk validation slice, not the architectural boundary.
- Project Owner approved Native Architecture-First on 2026-09-15. Native AgentBridge design and, after the applicable architecture gates, implementation are now the project direction. The ordinary existential `native/profile/upstream/stop` decision is superseded; reviews may block or redesign a specific architecture, capability, implementation, release, or claim, but do not repeatedly reopen whether to begin the project.
- Existing protocols are evidence, sources of mechanisms and defects, comparison references, and optional fail-closed edge integrations, not mandatory runtime dependencies.
- Rust is an implementation hypothesis only. Language, transport, wire/content formats, security profiles, and repository structure must be selected later from requirements, threat modeling, conformance prototypes, benchmarks, and operational constraints.
- Frozen EI-01–EI-25, SBC-01–SBC-10 and retained safety, evidence, independence, IP, storage, and publication policies remain non-weakenable architecture constraints.
- AA-1 Architecture Constitution passed on 2026-09-16. The current next step is documentary AA-2 Abstract Normative Model (AD-4–AD-7): roles/context, authority/delegation/consent/effect/evidence, lifecycle/retry/replay/cancel/revoke/compensation, and proof obligations. Executable models/prototypes remain blocked until exact AD-21 controls are frozen and approved, and reference implementation remains blocked until AA-6.
- Business strategy is a parallel but separate track: an open royalty-free neutral Core plus a separate founder-owned commercial company offering voluntary replaceable services. Initial Business & Ecosystem Strategy and Protocol/Platform Firewall are required before paid design-partner work, external contributions, public preview, company/foundation formation, or protocol-asset transfer.
